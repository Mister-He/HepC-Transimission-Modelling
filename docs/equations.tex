\subsection{Effective Rate Definitions}

The population-entry term is piecewise in model time. The state at model year
$t=150$ is the equilibrium snapshot mapped to calendar year 2015. Before that
snapshot, $\beta_i(t)=b_i\,s_i$, where $b_i$ is the 2015 SingStat/CNB
age-specific new-drug-abuser count and $s_i$ is the calibrated age-specific
scale. For calendar years 2016--2025, $\beta_i(t)=b_i(y)\,s_i$ uses the
corresponding SingStat/CNB count; after the latest observed year,
$b_i(y)=b_i(2025)$ through 2030. This schedule affects the inflow into
$D_{u,1,i}$ only.

To account for genotype-3 excess risk, define the effective progression 
rates for each transition as weighted composites across genotypes:
\begin{align*}
\tilde{p}_{c,1\to2} &= \rho\,r_3^{\mathrm{NC\to CC}}\,p_3^{\mathrm{NC\to CC}} 
                      + (1-\rho)\,p_3^{\mathrm{NC\to CC}} \\
\tilde{p}_{c,2\to3} &= \rho\,r_3^{\mathrm{CC\to DC}}\,p_3^{\mathrm{CC\to DC}} 
                      + (1-\rho)\,p_3^{\mathrm{CC\to DC}} \\
\tilde{p}_{c,2\to4} &= \rho\,r_3^{\mathrm{CC\to HCC}}\,p_3^{\mathrm{CC\to HCC}} 
                      + (1-\rho)\,p_3^{\mathrm{CC\to HCC}} \\
\tilde{p}_{c,3\to4} &= \rho\,r_3^{\mathrm{DC\to HCC}}\,p_3^{\mathrm{DC\to HCC}} 
                      + (1-\rho)\,p_3^{\mathrm{DC\to HCC}}
\end{align*}

\noindent and the post-SVR effective progression rates for recovered individuals:
\begin{align*}
\tilde{p}_{r,2\to3} &= \phi^{\mathrm{CC\to DC}}\,\tilde{p}_{c,2\to3} \\
\tilde{p}_{r,2\to4} &= \phi^{\mathrm{CC\to HCC}}\,\tilde{p}_{c,2\to4} \\
\tilde{p}_{r,3\to4} &= \phi^{\mathrm{DC\to HCC}}\,\tilde{p}_{c,3\to4}
\end{align*}

\noindent The five HCV states are $u$ (susceptible/uninfected), $a$ (acute
infection), $c$ (chronic infection), $t$ (on treatment), and $r$ (recovered).
The state transitions are
\begin{align*}
a &\xrightarrow{\kappa/\iota_1} r, \\
a &\xrightarrow{(1-\kappa)/\iota_1} c, \\
c &\xrightarrow{\tau_k} t, \\
t &\xrightarrow{\bar\alpha_k/\iota_2} r, \\
t &\xrightarrow{(1-\bar\alpha_k)/\iota_2} c, \\
r &\xrightarrow{\gamma_{i,j}(t)} a.
\end{align*}
For the active injecting strata $D$ and $F$, recovered individuals are exposed
to reinfection. In accordance with the existing model, $J$ and $X$ do not have
a transmission term because they are not active injectors.

\noindent The effective SVR12 rate at each stage accounts for the genotype 
mix:
\begin{align*}
\bar{\alpha}_1 &= \rho\,\alpha_3^{\mathrm{NC}} + (1-\rho)\,\alpha_o^{\mathrm{NC}} \\
\bar{\alpha}_3^{\pm} &= \rho\,\alpha_3^{\mathrm{DC}\pm} + (1-\rho)\,\alpha_o^{\mathrm{DC}\pm}
\end{align*}

\noindent For stages 1--2 (non-cirrhosis and compensated cirrhosis), 
$\bar{\alpha} \equiv \bar{\alpha}_1$. For stage 3 (decompensated cirrhosis), 
$\bar{\alpha} \equiv \bar{\alpha}_3^{+}$ (RBV+) or $\bar{\alpha}_3^{-}$ 
(RBV-free) depending on the treatment scenario. For stage 4 (HCC), 
we assume $\bar{\alpha} \equiv \bar{\alpha}_3^{+}$ as a conservative 
approximation. \\[1em]

\noindent The effective disease-specific mortality rates incorporating 
SVR benefit are:
\begin{align*}
\omega\mu^{\mathrm{DC}}_{\mathrm{SVR}} &= \psi^{\mathrm{DC}}\,(\omega\mu^{\mathrm{DC}}+\omega\mu_i) \\
\omega\mu^{\mathrm{HCC}}_{\mathrm{SVR}} &= \psi^{\mathrm{HCC}}\,(\omega\mu^{\mathrm{HCC}}+\omega\mu_i)
\end{align*}
