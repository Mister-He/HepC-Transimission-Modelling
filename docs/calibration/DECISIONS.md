## 2026-08-08 — Progression rates: highest defensible literature values

- Change considered: `p_CC_DC`, `p_CC_HCC`, `p_DC_HCC` in `src/setup.R`.
- Reason: user directed to re-select progression rates with the highest
  justifiable transition probabilities. Previous values (0.039, 0.014,
  0.014 /yr) were below the HCV-cirrhosis natural-history literature
  (they implied ~19 yr in compensated cirrhosis and ~71 yr from
  decompensation to HCC).
- Evidence:
  - Alazawi et al. 2010, Aliment Pharmacol Ther 32(3):344-55, PMID
    20497143 (systematic review, 13 studies, 2386 HCV-monoinfected
    compensated-cirrhosis patients): decompensation 6.37%/yr pooled,
    7.88%/yr in untreated-only studies; HCC 3.36%/yr pooled, 4.79%/yr in
    untreated-only studies. The model has no treatment (tau = 0), so the
    untreated-only rates are the relevant upper bounds.
  - Rivera-Irigoin et al. 2006, AIDS Res Hum Retroviruses 22(12):1236-41,
    PMID 17209765: HCC in HCV-monoinfected decompensated cirrhosis 3.31
    per 100 person-years, 95% CI 2.70-4.64; upper bound used for
    `p_DC_HCC`.
- Decision: adopt `p_CC_DC = 0.0788`, `p_CC_HCC = 0.0479`,
  `p_DC_HCC = 0.0464` /yr. Genotype-weighted effective rates
  (`rho = 0.78`, GT3 RRs 1.36/1.36/1.93/1.93) are CC->DC 0.101/yr,
  CC->HCC 0.083/yr, DC->HCC 0.080/yr — at/above the upper end of the
  literature ranges for GT3-dominant populations; documented in
  `natural_history_review.md`.

## 2026-08-08 — beta_scale > 1 outcome (soft rule)

- Result: the 12-parameter fit that meets all acceptance criteria has
  beta_scale = (0.169, 0.911, 1.283, 5.435, 15.135, 112.264). Groups 1-2
  (<20, 20-29) are below 1; groups 3-6 are above 1.
- Reason: the <20 and 20-29 prison populations (99 and 1244) are small,
  while the CNB-anchored base beta values are large (257 and 644/yr); the
  steady-state balance needs net inflows of ~43 and ~587/yr respectively,
  i.e. scales of 0.17 and 0.91. Forcing scales >= 1 would require inflows
  >= 257 and >= 644/yr and would overshoot the young prison populations
  several-fold (documented by the constrained probe
  output/calibration/probe_beta_constraint_*/beta_constraint_compare.csv).
- Decision: per the user's soft rule, keep the unconstrained fit; the rule
  is abandoned for groups 1-2 with this documented reason. Absolute
  modelled inflows: ~43, ~587, ~375, ~511, ~318, ~112/yr (total ~1946/yr,
  exceeding the CNB-anchored base total of 1309/yr).
